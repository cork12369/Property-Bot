# Co-Living Checklist

Scoring rubric for co-living property evaluation.
Source: `Requirements/photo_2026-09-12_17-05-38.jpg` (OCR + manual corrections).

## Scoring table

| # | Criterion | 4 points | 3 points | 2 points | 1 point |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proximity to MRT Lines | Walking time within 3 min | Walking time within 3–5 min | Walking time within 5–7 min | Walking time more than 7 min |
| 2 | Proximity to Shopping Mall | Walking time within 3 min | Walking time within 3–5 min | Walking time within 5–7 min | Walking time more than 7 min |
| 3 | Tenant Mix in Shopping Mall | Shopping mall with cinema, library, grocery store and food court | Small shopping mall with grocery store and food court | Big neighbourhood retailers | Small neighbourhood retailers |
| 4 | Number of Units in the Project | 500 to 700 | 700 to 1000 units | Between 300 to 500 or 1000 to 1300 | Less than 300 or more than 1300 units |
| 5 | Availability of Facilities in the Project | Yes — tennis court, gym and lap pool | Yes — gym and lap pool | Yes — gym or swimming pool only | No — no facility |
| 6 | Rental Demand in the Area | Very strong (within business parks & schools …) | Strong (within business park or universities) | Ripple effect from business park and universities | Weak (no strong rental demand) |
| 7 | MRT Lines | Green Line (East West Line) | Blue, Purple and Brown lines (Downtown, North-East Line & East Coast Line) | Yellow Line (Circle Line) | Red Line (North-South Line) |
| 8 | Closing Price vs Past Transactions | More than 5% below past average transaction | Less than 5% below average transaction | According to past average transaction | Above average transaction |

## Outcome

Score = total points / 8

- `> 3.5` = **GREAT**
- `> 3` = **GOOD**
- `> 2.5` = **OK**
